Found before anything is copied: identical files, same ASIN/ISBN, same or similar title by the same author, or an audiobook with the same author and running time. "Book 1" never pairs with "Book 2". The highlighted copy is the recommended keeper (best format, bitrate, chapters, cover, fewest parts). Different ebook formats of one book are merged into one folder, the way Calibre wants them.
high = identical after normalising (accents, initials, "Last, First") - safe. medium = likely typo. low = look carefully ("Robert Jordan" vs "Robert Jordan Jr."). glued = several authors in one name. Approved merges become permanent aliases, applied on every future scan; rejections are remembered too.
